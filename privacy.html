01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tank Calculator (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit TankCalculator.net and use our tank volume and weight calculators.

The short version: the numbers you enter — tank shape, dimensions, head type, fill depth, liquid or custom density, tilt, and unit system — are computed entirely by JavaScript running in your browser. They are never transmitted to, stored on, or even seen by our servers. We collect only the standard, anonymized analytics any website needs to stay online and improve.

Never collected

Dimensions, head types, fill depths, liquid choices, custom densities — computed in your tab, gone when you close it.

Collected in aggregate

Page views, referrers, approximate region, device type — the anonymous traffic data every site needs to operate.

Always yours

Opt out of personalized ads any time, request deletion of anything tied to an email you sent, no hoops.

Never sold

No behavioral profiles, no data sales, no cross-site tracking — there is no behavioral data to sell, because inputs never leave your device.

How a Calculation Travels

The whole privacy promise in three stops — drawn, because it is shorter than the paragraphs.

Your Keyboard

You type the tank shape, dimensions, head type, fill depth, liquid or custom density, tilt, and unit system into the page.

→

Your Browser

JavaScript in your own tab runs the shape geometry, the dish-end corrections, the dip-chart integrator, the density × volume math, and the tilt correction. No network request is made.

→

Nowhere Else

The results appear on your screen and stay there. There is no upload step, no input store, and no log of your numbers anywhere on our side.

02Your Calculator Inputs

Every number you type into the calculator is a local event. The engine — the geometry for 10 tank shapes, the dish-end corrections (flat, hemispherical, 2:1 elliptical, torispherical), the dip-chart integrator, the liquid density and weight conversion, the tilt correction, and unit conversion — runs on your device, in your browser tab, with no network call attached to it.

We do not transmit, log, aggregate, or read your gauge values. There is no “recent calculations” history on our side to subpoena, sell, or leak, because none exists. If you close the tab, the inputs are gone unless your browser itself kept a locally stored preference for convenience (see Cookies & Local Storage below).

What We Never See, Store, or Transmit

  • ✓Tank dimensions, head types, and fill depth values
  • ✓Shape presets, head selections, and liquid choices
  • ✓Custom density values and tilt angles
  • ✓Unit system and display preferences
  • ✓Dip charts, strapping tables, or uploaded drawings
  • ✓Payment details — there is nothing to pay for

03Information We Collect

Server Logs & Technical Data

When you request any page, our hosting layer may automatically record:

  • Your IP address (used only for approximate country/region and abuse prevention, never to identify you)
  • Browser type, version, and device category
  • Pages requested, timestamps, and response sizes
  • The referring site or search query that brought you here

Messages You Send Us

If you email support or submit a correction, we receive whatever you choose to include — for example, the inputs you were testing, a screenshot of the result panel, and your email address so we can reply. We use it solely to answer you and to fix whatever you reported.

04Cookies & Local Storage

We use a minimum of browser storage, and only for two purposes:

  • Site functionality: remembering interface preferences (such as your last-selected unit system or advanced-options panel state) so the calculator feels continuous between visits.
  • Advertising & analytics: third-party cookies from our ad and analytics providers, described in the sections below.

You can clear or block all cookies in your browser settings; the calculator will still work, because none of the math depends on cookies.

05Advertising

This site is free because advertising pays for it. We use third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve ads. These vendors may use cookies (including the Google advertising cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings , or opt out of many third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info .
  • Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain U.S. states), a consent banner lets you accept or reject personalized advertising before ad scripts load.

Ads are clearly separated from calculator output and editorial content. We do not control which ads appear and do not endorse advertised products.

06Analytics

We use privacy-conscious, aggregate analytics to understand which tools and guides are useful — page views, session duration, referrers, approximate region, and device type. We do not attempt to identify individuals, and we do not build behavioral profiles across other websites.

07How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and secure the website and calculators
  • Improve tool accuracy, performance, and content coverage
  • Respond to support requests, corrections, and feedback you send us
  • Detect, prevent, and address abuse, scraping, or attacks
  • Display advertising that keeps the service free

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your calculator inputs for any purpose — again, because we never receive them.

08Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only in these narrow cases:

  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, and advertising vendors that process data on our behalf under contract
  • Legal requirements: when compelled by valid legal process from competent authorities
  • Site protection: to investigate abuse, attacks, or fraud affecting the service
  • Business transfer: if the site is ever merged or acquired, with notice via this page

09Data Retention & Security

Server logs are retained for a short operational window (typically 30–90 days) and then rotated or anonymized. Analytics data is kept in aggregate form. Emails you send us are retained only as long as needed to resolve your question and improve the tool, then deleted.

Because your calculation inputs never leave your device, the most sensitive data on this site has no server-side footprint to protect. For what little we do hold, we use HTTPS everywhere and reputable infrastructure providers. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but the architecture minimizes what could ever be exposed.

10Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), and similar regulations:

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising — which you can do any time via the ad opt-out links above or our consent banner
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

To exercise any right, email us with enough detail to verify your request. Because we hold so little personal data, most requests resolve within days.

11Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience — tank operators, process and building professionals, students, and adults planning home improvement projects — and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12Changes & Contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the site, vendors, or law. Material changes will be noted with a new “last updated” date at the top of this page, and the current version is always linked in the site footer.

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact page or email privacy@TankCalculator.net — we read everything.
